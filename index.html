About me

I'm a photographer specializing in portraits and street photography. Each frame is an opportunity to tell a story — yours, mine, or the city's. I'm currently looking for people to collaborate with: if you have an idea, a concept, or simply curiosity — get in touch and we'll bring it to life together. I'm open to collaborations and always eager to try something new.
